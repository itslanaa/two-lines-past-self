Two lines to my past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered.

Line one warns against pushing the Friday 5 p.m. deploy — the one-line fix deletes the production database. Line two traces the cause and effect: the rollback took all night, and I have never trusted a Friday deploy since.

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (185 characters, within the 1–280 gate).
